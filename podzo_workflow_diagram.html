From SAP S/4HANA Sales Order Intake to Multi-Driver Fleet Haulage, AI Vision Verification & MIRO Payment Clearance
Executive Overview What is the PODZO Project? (Explained in Plain English)
1 Live SAP Linkage: Connects heavy corporate SAP S/4HANA Sales Orders (VA01 / VL03N) directly with physical road freight and rail siding logistics.
2 Multi-Driver Fleet Dispatch: Transport carriers split heavy orders across multiple trucks and drivers using the standard 1 Box = 1 Ton (1,000 kg) payload rule.
3 Dual-Weighbridge Scale Auditing: Trucks are weighed at departure (mine siding) and arrival (customer yard) to catch any weight loss, leakage, or scale discrepancies.
4 Vision AI Document & Stamp Check: Drivers take a photo of the offloaded delivery note; our Vision AI extracts items, weights, and verifies the mandatory official receiving yard stamp.
5 Straight-Through SAP MIRO Posting: Clean deliveries immediately post the supplier invoice into SAP (MIRO) and clear customer payments—eliminating paper disputes and fraud.
